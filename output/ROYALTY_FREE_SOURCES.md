## Roblox Creator Store

The game already uses two licensed Pro Sound Effects billiard sounds from the Creator Store
(`rbxassetid://9113440781`, `9113442190`). That library is licensed for use in Roblox experiences,
so you can search it in Studio's Toolbox (Audio → "billiard"). Those sounds can't be downloaded
and processed by the tools here; they can only be used as they are, by ID.

## Not used: YouTube

YouTube's terms forbid downloading videos, and the audio in videos belongs to their creators.
Sounds cut from it, or made by closely copying it, would not be royalty-free.
